Continuous delivery - Scrum -- the next phase of Agile; DevOps - developer is responsible for everything end-to-end. What technical writers develop is a side product that the software developers develop (MVP).

Maintenance costs is too high in technical writing.

Delivery channels of technical content : Help Portals, Printed Instructions on Paper, Video Delivery Channels (for example, youtube, vimeo), Artificial Intelligent Chatbots (Siri, Alexa)

As a technical writer, you need to design and define the offering for the user.

	Identify the following:
		Target user: Who, what, what do they intend to achieve?
		Information offering: Format of consumption (print/paper); is the user inclined towards reading or watching videos or the combination; assess their work environment in which they would use your user assistance information block.
		Possible delivery channels: How do we reach the user? What device do they use?
