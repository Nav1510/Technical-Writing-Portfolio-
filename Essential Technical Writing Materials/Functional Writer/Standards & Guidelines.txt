Standards & Guidelines:

River-raft concept : write down everything as an endless flow of text.
The STOP (Sequential Thematic Organization of Proposals) concept : Write down things in a way it can be consumed as smaller units rather than the entire flow.

Why do we need structure in writing?

Technical writing is not only about language. Most aspects of technical writing are well addressed by structured writing.

Organize volumes of material; writing is small vs. large companies is a different ball game; have a mindful structured writing approach will increase your chances of working in large writing teams.
